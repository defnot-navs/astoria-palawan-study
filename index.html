About

The resort, and why we studied it

Hospitality concept

Astoria Palawan is a resort in Puerto Princesa that “kisses the shores of the majestic Sulu Sea.” It is promoted as a family-centric, eco-friendly resort surrounded by lush landscapes, and as a quiet escape from the city.

According to its official About page, it uses green materials and practices, including VRF air conditioning and solar panels that the resort says save up to 40% on electricity.

Facilities, per the official site

A 39-meter infinity pool, a recreation pavilion with karaoke and games, in-house restaurants and a bar, the Mangrove Conference and Convention Center, and the one-hectare Palawan Waterpark.

About this project

This website is a tourism-class case study. It borrows the structure of a real resort website (hero, accommodations, amenities, dining, offers, contact) and rebuilds it as an original design with its own code, wording and layout. Photos are generic stock images, not the resort's photography.

Getting there per official About page, confirm before travel

Exclusive van transfer

₱4,500 per way per van (up to 8 guests) from Puerto Princesa Airport. Reserve at least 72 hours ahead by email.

Shared shuttle

₱700 per person per way. Reserve at least 72 hours ahead by email.

City shuttle

₱250 per person per way between the resort and the Astoria Palawan Sales Office at Mercado de San Miguel, near the airport.

Senior citizens, persons with disabilities and students are listed as getting 20% off the city and shared shuttle fares with a valid ID. Schedules change: always confirm with the resort.

Frequently asked questions

The resort's own FAQ page was empty when checked, so these answers use verified details from its Home, About and Contact pages.

How far is the resort from the airport?

About 90 minutes from Puerto Princesa International Airport, per the official site.

How do I get there?

Options listed on the official About page include an exclusive van transfer, shared shuttle, city shuttle, taxi or van, or a bus toward Roxas or El Nido that passes the resort. See the Getting There section above and confirm with the resort.

What are the reservations office hours?

8AM to 6PM.

What are the pool hours?

The Pod, Premier Pod and Nestling Pool are listed as open 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM.

Where can I see real room rates?

Use "View Rates" or "Check Availability" on astoriapalawan.com, or the official booking site. Prices on this project are illustrative.

Does this website take bookings?

No. It is a static school project. Its forms are demonstrations only.
